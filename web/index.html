Resume Fit Analyzer

Scores your resume against a job description requirement by requirement, and tells you exactly which skills are missing.

Runs entirely in your browser — your resume is never uploaded anywhere.

Drop a PDF here, or browse

What you get

  • An overall fit score blending literal skill matching with semantic similarity.
  • Requirement-by-requirement coverage — every bullet marked covered, partial or missing, with the line from your resume that matched it.
  • A concrete skill gap list, split into must-haves and nice-to-haves, including related experience you already have.
  • ATS readability checks beyond a word count.

The first analysis downloads about 30 MB to your browser — a 22.6 MB language model plus the runtime that executes it — and caches both. Everything after that is instant and works offline.

Skill coverage — Technologies named in the posting that appear in your resume.
Requirements met — How well your resume evidences each requirement.
Must-have gaps — Required skills with no evidence in your resume.
